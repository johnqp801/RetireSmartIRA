## Promotional Text (170 char max) — 156 chars — FINAL/APPROVED

```
Your year-round retirement tax planner. Map a Roth conversion strategy across your whole retirement and see what it costs in tax, IRMAA and Social Security.
```

**Why it changed.** The prior text (approved for 2.0.0, reused for 2.0.1 and 2.1.0, 146 chars) read:

```
Your year-round retirement tax planner, now with a Multi-Year Plan that maps a Roth conversion strategy across retirement and shows what it saves.
```

Still accurate, but "now with" had gone stale: Multi-Year shipped in 2.0.0, three releases back, so in a 2.3 listing it read as though nothing had moved since. Promotional text is also the one field editable without a build, so refreshing it carries no submission risk.

**Deliberately evergreen.** It sells the app rather than this release, because promotional text sits above the description and is read by people who have not installed yet. It should not need rewriting for 2.4.

**Positioning checks passed:** no "recommends" and nothing implying advice (the app is a companion tool and makes no recommendations, per decisions/log.md 2026-07-11); no claim of a selectable optimizer objective, which still does not exist.

Map a Roth conversion strategy across retirement, and now choose how each year's tax gets paid: withheld from the conversion, from your accounts, or outside money.

See how a Roth conversion strategy plays out across your whole retirement, year by year, before you commit to the first one. Your numbers, not a rule of thumb.

The live description is 2.1.0's. Its MULTI-YEAR TAX PLAN section leads with the selectable/comparable conversion approaches and says nothing about choosing how conversion tax is funded, which is the headline of this release and the thing promised to a customer.

Not required for submission and not changed here, since only the promotional text was in scope.
