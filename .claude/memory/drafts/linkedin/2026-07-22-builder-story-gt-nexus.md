## Draft text

I sold my last software company to Infor in 2015. GT Nexus was a supply-chain platform with thousands of enterprises moving real goods through it. Then I spent a few years planning my own retirement and could not find a tax tool I trusted.

None of them showed their work. The web planners were shallow on the mechanics that actually move the number: IRMAA, the ACA cliff, the survivor filing-status jump, inherited IRA rules. The brokerage tools were built by firms that sell products. Each one handed me a result and asked me to take it on faith.

So I built the one I wanted. RetireSmartIRA models Roth conversions, Social Security timing, RMDs, and Medicare surcharges together, for this year and across retirement. Every number traces back to the input behind it. Disagree with a result and you can open it and see exactly where it came from.

iPhone, iPad, and Mac. Nothing links to your accounts, nothing leaves your device. Free right now.

What retirement number do you most wish you could see the math behind?
