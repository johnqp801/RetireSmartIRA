# V2.4.1: accuracy page follow-ups

Parked 2026-08-06 while 2.4.0 build 64 is in Apple review. Three items, in this order.

## 1. The tax year is derivable and must not read as unknown. FIX FIRST.

Minnesota renders **"Minnesota tax treatment, tax year not recorded"** above precise brackets and a
$30,600 standard deduction. The figures come from `statetax-2026-MN.json`, loaded from the `2026/`
directory, so the app knows exactly what tax year they are. The blank exists only because the page
reads `verification.taxYear`, which was populated for 15 states and left at `0` for the other 36.

**The result is the worst combination available:** confident numbers headed by a statement that we do
not know what year they describe. A reader concludes the figures are provisional. They are not.

**Fix:** derive the tax year from the loaded config set rather than from the verification block. The
year is a property of the DATA; the verification date is a property of the CHECKING we did. Conflating
them was a design error. Independent of items 2 and 3, so it lands even if they slip.

## 2. Approved copy for unstamped states. Option A, approved by John 2026-08-06.

Replaces "No verification date recorded." / "No primary sources recorded." on the 36 states without
verification metadata, once item 1 supplies the year:

> Minnesota tax treatment, 2026
> These figures were derived from Minnesota's published tax forms and instructions and are covered by
> our test suite. We have not yet published the verification date and source links for this state.

Why this wording, so a later editor does not flatten it:
- **"Derived from" rather than "verified against" or "well researched".** Derived is what actually
  happened and it is checkable. An adjective about effort, asserted about your own work, does less
  than the plain description of provenance.
- **"Covered by our test suite" rather than any accuracy claim.** True, unusual enough to mean
  something, and it promises nothing about correctness.
- **"Not yet published" rather than "not recorded".** The current wording describes internal
  bookkeeping. "Not yet published" tells the user this is coming, which item 3 delivers.

**THIS COPY MUST NEVER RENDER FOR A STATE THAT DID NOT GO THROUGH THE PHASE 4 AUDIT.** Montana has no
golden fixture. If Montana renders this sentence it is FALSE. See item 3.

**Two decisions this task needs before it runs:** some fixtures carry several `sourceURL`s across

Effort is closer to one reviewed task than to a phase.

## Standing note for user replies

**When answering a user or tester who asks whether the state data is accurate, use the substance of
item 2's language.** The honest shape, in three parts:

1. Figures come from each state's own published forms and instructions, and each is pinned by a test
   that fails if it drifts.
2. Where the app knows it cannot model a rule, the state's page says so rather than quietly producing
   a number.
3. What CANNOT be claimed: that every state has been independently re-verified recently. That is true
   of 15 states today, not 51.

**Never say the blanks mean a state has not updated recently.** They mean WE have not published a
verification date. Nothing on that page speaks to state legislative activity.

**Never let an empty limitations list read as a clean bill.** Empty means nothing has been RECORDED,
not that nothing is wrong. 88 defect cases across 29 jurisdictions remain open.
