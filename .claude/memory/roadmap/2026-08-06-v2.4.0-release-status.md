## What shipped

All six commitments made in writing to Steve Nicolai and Alan Levy on 2026-08-03, the first time any
of them has reached users:

1. Kansas personal exemption (Phase 5a)
2. Iowa exclusion including Roth conversions (Phase 5a)
3. Per-state accuracy page, Steve's suggestion F
4. RMD summary leads with whoever reaches RMD age first
5. RMD chart separates the two people
6. Caret at end of pre-filled numeric fields, owed to Alan since 2026-07-19

Plus State Tax Phase 5b: 99 defect cases across 32 jurisdictions down to 88 across 29, with per-source
rules for Kansas, Massachusetts, Arizona and DC, and four jurisdictions (Hawaii, North Carolina, Idaho,
Vermont) deliberately shipping no rule with guards and on-screen disclosures instead.
